Focus stack one sharp picture from many focus steps

  1. Focus on the nearest part of the subject.
  2. Press Start, then turn the focus knob slowly and steadily until the farthest part has been sharp.
  3. Press Fuse. Frames are kept only when the focus has moved, so pauses don't matter.
